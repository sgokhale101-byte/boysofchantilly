Explain It: projections

Each starter's ESPN projection is adjusted with the Vegas line for their NFL game: players on teams Vegas expects to score more get a bump, and players facing low-scoring games get trimmed. A defense gets the reverse, based on its opponent's expected points. Points already scored are locked in, and only the unplayed part of each game is projected.

Win chances come from the gap between the two projected totals and how much each lineup still has left to play: the more points still on the table, the more uncertain the result.

    Hall of Fame

    Explain It

    League records across every season on record. Scores, weeks, and streaks count regular-season games. Streaks follow a manager across seasons. Median streaks start in 2022. Luck lists use finished seasons; luck is actual head-to-head wins minus the wins a team's scores earned against the whole league each week. Accuracy lists use finished seasons with weekly lineup data (at least 6 weeks). Season lists rank finished seasons by median points per game (the middle weekly score), so one huge or tiny week doesn't swing a season. Player performances use your league's scoring and include bench players, marked as benched. The dropdown switches every list between all time, the Plumber era (through 2021), the Median era (2022 on), and single seasons. Streaks only count games inside the chosen span. Median streaks show for the Median era and seasons from 2022 on.

    Manager profiles

    Explain It

    Every manager who has played in the league, current and former, across every season ESPN has on record. Weeks and head-to-head records count regular-season games. Cakewalk is the manager they beat most often, Their Daddy is the one who owns them, and Rival is their closest head-to-head series. For current managers, all three are other current managers. Luck index: actual head-to-head wins minus the wins your scores earned against the whole league each week (the all-play record). Positive means the schedule has helped, negative means it has hurt. Bad beats are losses while scoring above that week's median; lucky wins are wins while scoring below it.

    Trade Review

    Explain It

    Every trade ESPN has on record. This season's trades are just listed. For finished seasons, each side is scored by the starting-lineup points it got from the players it received, from the week of the trade through the fantasy playoffs (playoff bracket games count; consolation games don't). The side with more wins the trade; if the gap is under 15 points (or 15%), it's called even. Each side also shows how that team finished.

      FAAB Tracker

      Explain It

      Each team's FAAB left and spent. Vivek, Cameron, and Srimanth started with $110; everyone else started with the league budget.
